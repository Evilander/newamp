Key Facts
Formed in Bristol's Wild Bunch soundsystem collective; Robert Del Naja, Grant Marshall, and Andrew Vowles pioneered UK trip-hop.
Mezzanine (1998) was engineered by Neil McLellan and recorded partly at Real World Studios in Box, Wiltshire.
Del Naja is widely theorised to be Banksy — a claim neither confirmed nor denied.
The band famously released Heligoland stems for fan remixes, generating over 12,000 user-uploaded versions.
